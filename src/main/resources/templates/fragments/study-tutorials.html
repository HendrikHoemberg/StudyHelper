How it works

  • Each session surfaces the cards that are due today, plus up to a daily limit of new cards (20). Cards you already know are spaced further out and skipped until they come due.
  • Tap the card to flip between the question and the answer.
  • After revealing the answer, grade yourself with Again / Hard / Good / Easy. Each button previews when you'll next see the card (e.g. "Good · 6d").
  • Again re-queues the card later in the same session so you keep seeing it until it sticks; Hard, Good, and Easy schedule it for a future day and remove it from the session.

Tips

  • Grade honestly — the schedule only helps if it reflects what you actually recall.
  • Turn on the Practice toggle to run through cards without changing their schedule (good for last-minute cramming).
